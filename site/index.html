Your SDLC, automated.

OpenEngine takes a change and does the work around it that you never have time for — reviews it from every angle, works out what it can break, and shows you the system it lands in. What comes out the other side is a change you can ship.

Why

  • Not coupled to any provider.

    Claude Code, Codex, whatever comes next. The conversation is ours, not a vendor's, so any runner can pick up where another left off.

  • Workflows that encode your review standards.

    Stringent security reviews and checklists, defined once and applied to every change.

  • Work from your phone, on any project.

    Runs are durable and survive restarts, so you can start one anywhere and read the result later.

  • It augments your workflow; it doesn't replace it.

    You stay the one who decides what ships.
