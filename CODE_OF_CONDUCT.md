# Code of conduct

OpenEngine is a community of people who want to make software development
better. We want everyone who participates, whether in issues, pull requests,
Slack, or anywhere else the project meets, to feel welcome and safe doing so.

## Our standards

We expect participants to:

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Most friction is a misunderstanding, not malice.
- Give and accept feedback graciously. Reviews are about the change, not the
  author.
- Be mindful of the words you choose. Not everyone shares your context,
  language, or sense of humor.
- Make room for others. Welcome newcomers and help them succeed.

We will not tolerate:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind, including on the
  basis of gender, identity, orientation, disability, appearance, race,
  religion, age, or experience level.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including the GitHub repository,
Slack workspace, blog comments, and any event or channel where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at
[hello@openengine.cc](mailto:hello@openengine.cc). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers will review every report, respond to the reporter,
and take whatever action they consider appropriate, from a private warning to
removal from project spaces. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code
in good faith may face the same consequences.
